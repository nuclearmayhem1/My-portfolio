ABOUT

Game Programmer

I am a game programmer with a bachelor's degree in Games & Entertainment Technology, with a particular interest in C#, systems programming, and game architecture. I enjoy working with complex systems and designing architectures to support them.

My project, Author Arcana, explores integrating an Entity Component System (ECS) architecture into Godot. It serves as an opportunity to explore ECS implementation and its potential applications in game development.

I have a passion for complex games, both playing and developing them, and am particularly interested in engine and systems programming. My approach to problem-solving starts with the desired outcome: working backwards to identify the necessary steps and learning what I need along the way.
